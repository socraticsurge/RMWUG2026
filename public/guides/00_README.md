# Artifact Research and Edited-Volume Kit

**Working volume:** *Commerce Through Digital Artifacts: Eighty Structured Studies of Contemporary Indian Commerce*  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Standards checked:** 10 August 2026

## What this kit is for

This kit turns a 6–8 hour research-methods laboratory into a coordinated artifact-research programme. Each of approximately 80 second-year Commerce students produces one short, evidence-traceable research note. The notes use different topics but share one method, one manuscript structure and one editorial standard. They can therefore be compiled into a coherent workshop proceedings volume.

The intended same-day output is **Workshop Proceedings v0.9**, not an automatically public final publication. Public release as v1.0 requires the post-workshop editorial, consent, copyright and evidence checks in this kit.

## Non-negotiable method

Every note must:

1. register a provisional claim and a plausible rival;
2. define an artifact population and sampling rule before collection;
3. treat absence and uncertainty as data rather than replacing inconvenient cases;
4. preserve the URL, retrieval date and evidence location for every coded artifact;
5. distinguish AI-proposed extraction from human-accepted data;
6. limit its claim to what the sample and design support;
7. disclose material AI assistance; and
8. pass evidence, manuscript and publication checks before compilation.

## Recommended use sequence

### Before the workshop

1. Approve the volume title, scope, institutional publication route and student-consent language.
2. Select or adapt 80 topics from the bank.
3. Preflight every topic for accessible artifacts and obvious ethical or legal problems.
4. Allocate one topic per student and freeze the topic register.
5. Prepare the LaTeX master and test one sample note end to end.

### During the workshop

Students work through the seven gates in the workbook. Facilitators accept or return each gate. A student may proceed only after the dependency needed for the next stage is adequate.

### After the workshop

Compile v0.9, complete the editorial review, obtain final author confirmation, resolve copyright and evidence problems, and only then authorize v1.0 for public release.

## Authority and local policy

This kit is a teaching and editorial framework, not legal advice. Institutional policy, research-ethics requirements, applicable law, platform terms, publisher rules and the final editor’s decisions take precedence. Tool capabilities and policies can change; recheck them before delivery.
